# Small victories

29 bilingual milestones cover work, physical preparation, calm, mentors, learned techniques, practical methods, research, deliveries, peaceful solutions and the next visible chapter. The hero panel shows three relevant unfinished goals and folds completed milestones into a collection.

Achievements are awarded from successful engine actions or actual elapsed-time progress. Starting, cancelling or merely finishing an unpaid contract does not count as turning it in. Failed combat and declined offers do not count as peaceful victories. Special-method counts increase only after accepted technique actions.

Reliable Shoulders unlocks careful carrying in an active local hauling contract: one contract step, ordinary hauling practice, 3 stamina and a 60-second recovery. From Lesson to Habit unlocks a short practice session after five special-method uses: up to five actual practice, 5 stamina total and a 60-second recovery. Both interrupt meditation, respect travel/work context, mastery limits and movement-only physical growth. There are no extra resources for repeatedly claiming a badge.

The collection survives death and rebirth; current-life usage counters and recovery timers reset on rebirth. Old saves recover milestones only from provable state. Heavy-contract, invoice-contract and recovery-contract completion are not inferred from a generic completed-contract total. Viewing goals uses no random draws and never grants a reward.

Online and offline milestones share the same completion semantics. Notifications are displayed for newly recorded milestones; historical achievements are not repeatedly announced when loading a save.
