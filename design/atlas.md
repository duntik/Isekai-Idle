# Map as an exploration journal

Only known locations and the immediately following chapter goal are shown. Rumours derive from actual story progress and mentors, carry a source, and become confirmed local routes after two safe surveys. They never bypass entry restrictions or dangerous frontier prerequisites.

Surveys require the current encounter to be resolved, no travel, 15 stamina and a 300-second recovery. They interrupt meditation, describe only safe outer areas and grant small local supplies. Two local surveys find a trail: travel to that location takes 80% of the usual time. Later surveys remain useful; no surveys run automatically while offline. Offline time only restores recovery.

Generated pending tasks and causal continuations appear as marked map nodes. Their details and search actions require the correct location and existing encounter cooldown. Resolved causal nodes remain visible when bookmarked or annotated. The current unresolved encounter has a separate return control.

Personal notes use a persistent textarea outside the refreshed map canvas. Up to 600 characters are stored as plain text in the save; note text never becomes HTML or a translated game message. Updating the map, changing zoom or toggling its filter preserves a note draft and map position. Bookmark and note operations do not interrupt cultivation or draw random numbers.

The map offers zoom, drag, centring, overview, pending/bookmarked filtering, safe/danger descriptions and found-trail indicators. Desktop has a side journal; smaller screens stack the journal below the map. Both visual and data interfaces share the mechanics and native RU/EN explanations.
