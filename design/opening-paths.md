# First-chapter local apprenticeships

The opening has three optional, compatible threads. They describe opportunities until the player meets a person; no invisible quest is accepted automatically. A player can learn from every mentor in one run.

* Labor: meet Mei’s carters, learn safe lifting with Ren, train body 2, carry buckets locally, earn a supplies or stance-review opportunity.
* Herbal: meet Lin at the forest edge, identify leaves, provide five herbs for an infusion, return to Mei in the village for delivery and payment.
* Archive: meet Wei through the letter or paid archive visit, copy a symbol, gain calm 3, compare the temple’s safe outer wall, return to Wei with the observation.

All nine scenes have stable IDs, bilingual prose and once-only completion. Declining leaves the thread available; returning to the correct place is required. Rewards are ordinary herbs and money; mundane lessons never produce Qi. Technique practice benefits only an actually learned stance. Existing exploration remains available independently.

Early once-only lessons and first mentor introductions use a 90-second cooldown. Repeat trade and work retain their 300-second cooldown, so this does not accelerate repeated money farming. A first village encounter introduces Ren and Mei. Subsequent events remain seeded and varied; eligible meaningful authored opportunities become urgent after two missed offers in chapter zero. This reduces unlucky waiting without forcing a single story order. Later chapters retain their usual pacing.

`World.developmentPaths(state, language)` derives presentation from saved decisions and mentor contacts, with current next-step guidance and three-step progress. It adds no irreversible class or extra save state.
